Detailed Benefits & Financial Support • योजना के लाभ
expand_more

Under the scheme, financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year is released in three 4-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each directly into the bank accounts of eligible farmers' families across the country.

Apr - Jul ₹2,000
Aug - Nov ₹2,000
Dec - Mar ₹2,000

Exclusions & Disqualifications • अपवर्जन शर्तें
expand_more

The following categories of beneficiaries are not eligible for financial benefit under the scheme:

  • Institutional landholders.
  • Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government ministries/offices.
  • All superannuation/retirees pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000/- or more.
  • Persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
  • Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, etc.
