Document Safety Notice • दस्तावेज़ सुरक्षा

Yojana Saathi is an informational guide. We never ask you to upload documents, enter your Aadhaar number, or share OTPs. Carry these documents to the official portal.

Step-by-Step Official Application Steps

1

Access Official Portal

Navigate to pmkisan.gov.in or visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).

2

New Farmer Registration

In the Farmer Corner section, click 'New Farmer Registration'.

3

Aadhaar Authentication

Enter Aadhaar and mobile number, verify via official UIDAI OTP.

4

Enter Land & Personal Details

Input land survey/khata numbers and bank IFSC.

5

Receive Application ID

Save your registration reference number for installment status tracking.
